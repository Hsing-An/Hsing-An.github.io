About

Hsing-An Lee: Recorder Player / Composer

Born in Taiwan, graduated from the Department of Music at National Tsing Hua University, majoring in recorder and minoring in composition. Studied recorder with Meng-Heng Chen and composition with Hsin-Lei Chen and Chiu-Yu Chou.

Focused on early music and contemporary music in recorder performance, while continuing to study jazz music.
Recipient of awards in international competitions, with appearances as a soloist at the National Recital Hall and the National Taichung Theater.

As a composer, drawing from electronic music, jazz music, contemporary music, popular music, and traditional music.
Writing includes instrumental compositions as well as arrangements produced with music software. Awarded compositions have also been presented at Weiwuying.

Current areas of performance and creation:
  • Baroque instrumental performance
  • Renaissance instrumental performance
  • Renaissance consort performance
  • Jazz performance
  • Fusion-jazz composition and performance
  • Original and arranged works for recorder ensemble
  • Contemporary chamber music composition
  • Popular music composition and arrangement
